How it works

No app for your guests to install. One link, one QR code.

We shoot

Our camera sends every photo straight to your private event gallery during the event.

Guests scan

On the big wall screen

Every photo in the album plays on the big LED wall at your event, new ones the moment they arrive. Guests can add their own too.
